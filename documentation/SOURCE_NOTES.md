## Weight, coding and estimand corrections

ECLS C7WEIGHT is physical child weight in pounds. It is neither an analysis weight nor an eligibility criterion in the revision. Current child measures plus parent-derived W8SESL use the C7PW0 family (manual section 4.8.5.2, printed p.4-47), with C7CW0 as a child-only sensitivity. Each family supplies 90 JK2 replicates; variance is the sum of squared replicate deviations, without dividing by 90 (section 4.9.1, p.4-50). Normal-reference 95% intervals are used in the revision. Current weights do not automatically repair prior-grade response or complete-case selection.

W8SESL is standardized: ordinary negative values are valid and are retained; only −7/−8/−9 are missing. The support score uses at least four of five 1–5 items, higher means more frequent support. The main outcome reads the provider C7SDQINT composite directly, valid1–4, higher=more internalizing problems. Eight raw SDQ problem items supply sample alpha. No extra missing-item threshold is invented. The historical mixed outcome requires at least four of five1–4 items only for its explicitly labelled sensitivity. Two items come from SDQ and three from NELS self-concept/control prompts. Prior C5SDQINR is the official revised third-grade internalizing composite. Generic classmates wording need not align with English-class identifier J71CLASS.

TIMSS support is 5 minus the Q17 agreement mean, higher favorable; disorder is 5 minus the Q18 disruption frequency mean, higher disruption. Belonging and freedom from bullying are separate supplied scales. ICCS openness increases with reported frequency; reversing IS4G18H yields higher safety and reversing IS4G18I yields higher bullying fear.

Observed means are formed from valid reporter pools before outcome/covariate complete-case deletion, and are held fixed under inference. The conventional contextual difference is beta_B minus beta_W in the same common-unit model. It is an association contrast, not construct homogeneity, referent specificity, psychometric equivalence or a causal protective effect. LOO coefficients are conditional own/peer-report associations and are separately labeled. No external estimates are pooled or compared as common-scale effects.

## Historical files

The two `data_audit` JSON files are small aggregate records required by the scripts to reproduce the error-provenance ledger. They retain historical numbers but have explicit historical notices. The CEPS original status label and internally frozen block do **not** document public preregistration. The ECLS old diagnostic pools are not the final modeled-class reporter pool. Withdrawn body-weight analysis and old homogeneity interpretations must not be reused as current results.

## Public package changes

Only local path metadata and default path settings were replaced for distribution. Statistical equations, data-selection/scoring code, inference calculations, numerical output and input hashes were preserved. `package_manifest.json` records original and packaged hashes. Runtime-manifest output hashes identify original audited output bytes; packaged JSON hashes differ when their private path strings are redacted. The tidy aggregate CSV is byte-identical to the audited CSV. The consolidated final result JSON is authoritative; source-specific convenience files can omit metadata added only during consolidation.

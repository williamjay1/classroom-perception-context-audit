# Item content and official source map

## Text for the supplementary methods

The table identifies the content and survey location of the items used in the revised analyses. CEPS wording is reproduced in three short quotations; other item descriptions are paraphrases of official questionnaires or data documentation. Analysis fields in the CEPS public derivative are distinguished from questionnaire locations. The constructs differ in substantive content, response format and grouping as well as referent. Aggregating an item does not change what the item asks respondents to evaluate. Consequently these applications do not provide a controlled test of classroom versus school wording.

### CEPS baseline

| Analysis field | Official questionnaire location | Wording | Content and referent |
|---|---|---|---|
| classmategood | Student questionnaire C17, sixth statement, printed p.13 | “Most of my classmates are nice to me.” | Respondent's experience of classmates' treatment. |
| classgood | C17, eighth statement, printed p.13 | “My class is in good atmosphere.” | General appraisal of the class atmosphere. |
| friendly | C17, tenth statement, printed p.13 | “I feel close to people in this school.” | Personal affiliation with people at school. |

Source: official [Grade 7 student questionnaire](http://ceps.ruc.edu.cn/Enlish/dfiles/11184/14391213173188.pdf); the corresponding [Grade 9 questionnaire](http://ceps.ruc.edu.cn/Enlish/dfiles/11184/14391213594628.pdf) contains the same three C17 statements. The questionnaire presents statements in order rather than printing lettered subitem codes. The locations above therefore identify ordinal positions within C17; they do not invent raw-data variable names. The three quotations contain 25 words in total. Responses increase from disagreement to agreement on a 1–4 scale. The first two statements form the two-item class-related score. The third remains school belonging even when averaged within classes. Classmates' treatment, class atmosphere and school affiliation are different content domains.

## Confirmed revision corrections

The final ECLS extraction preserves valid negative W8SESL values and uses genuine C7PW0/C7PW replicate weights. Only W8SESL special codes −7, −8 and −9 are missing. The final ECLS official-outcome model is 3623 students in 538 English classes and 433 schools. Current and historical diagnostic pools are distinguished in the aggregate results. The main ECLS outcome is the official eight-item C7SDQINT, selected for formal scoring/content before seeing its new results. The prior researcher-defined mixed outcome is a common-sample measurement sensitivity.
